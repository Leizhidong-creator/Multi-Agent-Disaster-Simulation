GB 50016 Fire Safety Rules for Zhiyan Agent RAG

[GB50016-CH-003]
Title: Public building evacuation door width
Article: 5.5.18
Rule:
- Public building evacuation doors and corridors must keep adequate clear width.
- Doors in high-occupancy venues should not have thresholds.
- Net width for doors in crowded venues should not be less than 1.40 m.
- Evacuation corridors in public buildings should not be less than 1.10 m wide.

[GB50016-CH-004]
Title: Requirements for crowded public venues
Article: 5.5.19
Rule:
- Evacuation doors in crowded venues should not have thresholds.
- Outdoor evacuation passages should not be less than 3.00 m wide.
- The passage should connect directly to an open and spacious safe area.

[GB50016-CH-011]
Title: Width control and guardrails in stepped passages
Article: Explanatory note
Rule:
- Oversized stepped walkways increase instability and trampling risk.
- When a stepped passage is wider than 4 m, a guardrail should be installed every 2 m.
- Physical separation is a valid measure to reduce lateral force propagation.

[GB50016-CH-015]
Title: Public building evacuation distance
Article: 5.5.17
Rule:
- The direct distance from a room evacuation door to the nearest safe exit should remain within code limits.
- Staircases should connect directly to the outside whenever possible.
- When direct outdoor connection is difficult, the first floor should use an enlarged protected space.

[Design Notes for What-If Mitigation]
- Prioritize one-way splitting of opposing streams inside bottlenecks.
- Mark guardrail location, affected width and expected density reduction.
- Track before/after peak density, deadlock duration and slow-brain trigger count.
